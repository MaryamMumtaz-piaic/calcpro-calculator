How to use it

  1. 01Pick Standard or Scientific at the top of the calculator.
  2. 02Choose DEG or RAD before using trigonometry.
  3. 03Build an expression with the keypad or your keyboard. Parentheses auto-close when you press equals.
  4. 04Press =. The result is saved to history; click any history row to reuse it.

Supported syntax

+ − × ÷ ^ ( ) ! %, sin cos tan asin acos atan, sinh cosh tanh, ln log sqrt cbrt abs exp, floor ceil round, π e ans, decimals and scientific notation such as 1.5E3. Modulo is available as mod.

Understanding the modes

DEG measures angles in degrees (sin 30 = 0.5). RAD measures them in radians (sin π/2 = 1). Standard mode keeps the everyday keypad; your expression, memory and history carry over when you switch to Scientific. Everything is stored locally — clearing your browser data resets it.
